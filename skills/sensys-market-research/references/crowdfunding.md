# Product crowdfunding research

Default to relevant reward/preorder campaigns. The supplied screenshots identify
Kickstarter, Indiegogo, Makuake, CAMPFIRE, Wadiz, Zeczec, FlyingV and Ulule as leads.
GoFundMe (donation), Patreon (membership), Wefunder and Republic (investment) belong
to separate research modes. Verify each campaign's actual transaction model: some
platforms host multiple models. Geography labels in screenshots are leads, not a
current eligibility/legal determination.

1. Search the product category, synonyms, alternatives, local terms and delivery
   failures. Prefer campaigns comparable by category, region, reward price, phase
   and observation date. Seek unsuccessful/stalled/delayed cases as well as hits.
2. Capture campaign story, visible rewards, comments/Q&A and updates separately when
   they occupy different URLs. Record unavailable or authenticated sections.
3. Record platform, campaign URL/title/creator, audience, scene, promises, observation
   time, start/end dates, status/phase, target/pledged amounts, currency, backers,
   rewards/configurations, early-bird rules, quantity limits, shipping/tax, visible
   reward-level counts, expected delivery, delivery/refund evidence and source IDs.
   Preserve raw values and definitions; never interpret blank as zero. Mixed money
   units require a cited dated conversion, or comparison without aggregation.
4. Keep comments in context, including creator replies. Identify expectations,
   objections, willingness-to-pay clues, disappointed buyers, delays and workarounds.
5. Separate promotional attention, money commitments, actual delivery, retained use,
   repeat sales and profitability. Paid acquisition, existing fans, campaign targets,
   early-bird discounts and refund policies are confounders; mark them unknown when
   unavailable. Funding success alone proves none of product-market fit or profit.

Return a campaign comparison table with unknowns, evidence-backed observations,
counterexamples, limitations, and next validation. Missing comments/failed campaigns
make the demand assessment partial. Do not manufacture failure examples for balance.
Research does not authorize launching campaigns, accepting money or contacting backers.
