# Conditional research methods

Use methods to clarify an actual decision, not to fill every framework.

- **5W1H:** who, what, when, where, why and how; unknown fields remain unknown.
- **JTBD:** triggering circumstance, functional/emotional/social job, desired outcome,
  existing workaround and obstacle. A requested feature is not automatically the job.
- **POV:** evidence-backed population + need + explanatory insight; mark interpretation.
- **Journey:** trigger, discovery, evaluation, adoption, use, failure/abandonment and
  repeat use only where evidence exists. Unsupported emotions/stages are hypotheses.
- **HMW:** turn a demonstrated obstacle into an open design question, retaining scope.
- **Value proposition:** connect customer jobs/pains/gains with proposed services,
  pain relief and gain creation; distinguish observed needs from solution hypotheses.
- **Affinity mapping:** group observations while preserving source IDs and disagreement.
- **Opportunity prioritization:** explain urgency, alternatives, access to users,
  resources, confidence and a reversible next test; weights are judgments, not success odds.
- **Hardware concept tradeoffs / TRIZ:** optional when the user asks about translating
  needs into requirements or competing technical objectives. Market research does not
  prove engineering performance, supply readiness or manufacturing feasibility.

For validation choose the method for the unknown: observed diary use for frequency,
scenario interviews for context, behavior/purchase tests for willingness to pay,
usability tests for a design, and an appropriately sampled survey for prevalence.
Define recruitment, consent, duration, metrics, stopping criteria and a decision rule
before execution. Sample-size suggestions in upstream files are planning heuristics,
not statistical proof. Proposed interviews are not completed research.

For continuous recording or memory assistants, investigate episodic recording versus
all-day capture, useful recall versus transcription, contextual advice versus generic
chat, wear comfort, battery, subscription cost, deletion/export, bystander acceptance,
trust and adoption barriers. These are research dimensions, not predetermined findings.
Verify any actual legal requirement from current official sources for the intended
jurisdiction; do not present a generic worldwide recording rule.
