# Market sizing when the decision needs it

Define population, region, year, product category, purchaser, price, purchase frequency,
revenue versus units, replacement versus new demand, and reachable distribution.
Select primary statistics and specific research methodology. Preserve confidence
intervals or scenarios rather than pretending that uncertain inputs are precise.

Bottom-up: population x eligible segment x observed/assumed adoption x purchase frequency
x realized price. Label every assumption and check units. For subscription products,
separate hardware units from subscription revenue, conversion, retention and churn.
TAM, SAM and SOM must have explicit definitions and a reachable channel/resource basis.
Do not use universal SAM/TAM or SOM/SAM percentages. Social comment counts, pledges,
search traffic and overall AI industry revenue are not a valid product market base.

Return formulas, input sources, dates, denominators, scenarios, sensitivity and limits.
If the required base is unavailable, provide a measurement plan or clearly labeled
scenario calculation instead of a market estimate described as fact.
