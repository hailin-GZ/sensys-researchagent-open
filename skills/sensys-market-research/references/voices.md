# Public voices and supplied feedback

Use the bundled Comment Mining and Product Demand Research methods. Replace their
API-specific fetch step with actual public capture, authorized connector output,
or supplied exports. The bundled helper does not implement ScrapeCreators endpoints.

Keep a quote bank with post/product/project URL, comment locator, public timestamp,
necessary public profile signals, parent context, and exact text. Analyze jobs,
triggering events, current alternatives, pain, desired outcome, price objections,
adoption barriers, continued use and abandonment. Distinguish buyer, owner, prospective
buyer, reviewer, promoter and bystander; unknown role stays unknown.

Theme frequency uses a stated denominator among acquired observations and a defined
deduplication unit. It is not a population percentage. Separate unsolicited behavior
accounts from prompted preferences and from stated intention. A foreign community
can suggest hypotheses for a local market, but does not represent that market.

Use neutral scenario-first interview prompts. Learn about the last actual incident,
what the participant did, costs, alternatives and tradeoffs before describing the
proposed product. Avoid asking only whether someone likes the idea. Do not create
names, ages, incomes or diagnoses to make a persona vivid. Use evidence-backed
segments or explicitly provisional segment hypotheses with an experiment to test them.
