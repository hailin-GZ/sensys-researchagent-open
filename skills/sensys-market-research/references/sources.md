# Source roles and selection

Use `source-catalog.json` as leads, not an assertion of access or authority. Original
Chinese account names are source identifiers, not Chinese authored instructions.
Historical access tests are dated observations; fetch again when actually needed.

| Role | Preferred evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Official statistics / disclosure | Original dataset, definitions, public filings | Broad population is not product demand |
| Research institutions / commercial databases | Specific report with methodology, sample and date | Marketing and estimation may be embedded; some content is paid |
| Report discovery / aggregators | Index followed by original report | Syndicated reports count as one original origin |
| Media / WeChat / industry commentary | Original reporting and attributed opinions | Account directory, article discovery and archival are separate capabilities |
| Communities / reviews | Direct experiences with context and dated locators | Convenience samples, moderation, bots, incentives, and strong-opinion bias |
| Competitors / alternatives | Official feature, price and help pages plus independent experience | Vendor claims are claims; note currency, tax, subscription and availability |
| Crowdfunding | Campaign, rewards, backer voices, updates, delivery | See crowdfunding.md; funding is not recurring demand |
| Supplied research | Interviews, survey/export rows, notes and images | Preserve collection method and actual sample; do not fabricate study execution |
| Academic / patents | Original paper and methodology; patent for technical alternatives | Citation count, journal rank and patents do not establish customer demand |

For needs use primary voices and alternatives. For sizing use a defined statistical
base. For recent trends use timestamped signals. A narrowly scoped task may need only
one role; explain omissions that affect the decision. Include local-language discovery
for the target region; do not replace inaccessible domestic voices with foreign
enthusiast posts while claiming population coverage.

Search engines return leads. Capture the actual pages before using them for detailed
claims. Keep snippet-only evidence explicitly limited. Social APIs and authenticated
connectors are optional, and require actual configuration; no account/session extraction.
Single WeChat article capture does not implement account history or subscriptions.

When adding a new research capability or collector, first search existing recognized
workflows, inspect the actual implementation and license, and record reuse/adaptation
versus the gap requiring new work. Evaluate maintained code and local behavior, not
stars alone. Current optional candidates: Crawl4AI (dynamic self-hosted capture),
Firecrawl (search/capture workflow), baoyu URL-to-Markdown (interactive archive),
Docling (complex/OCR documents), last30days (recent signals), idea-reality-mcp
(software similarity). None is enabled merely by appearing in this list.
